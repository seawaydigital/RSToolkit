# Reporting toolkit security and content problems

Contact security.research@lakeheadu.ca privately. Operator: Andrew Austin at Lakehead University. Report the affected tool, version, observed behaviour and a minimal synthetic reproduction.

Do not post confidential research, personal allegations, credentials or detailed vulnerabilities in public issues. Do not send sensitive attachments until the appropriate recipient has agreed on a suitable channel. The toolkit does not provide emergency response or promise a universal legal protection for reports.

For an urgent institutional incident, use the institution's established incident process. Toolkit reporting does not replace it.

The candidate is independently prepared and is not an institutional security certification. See docs/operations-runbook.md for containment, correction, public notices and rollback. No response-time service level or backup operator is promised.
